Official Competition Rules

Effective: 11 October 2026. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

1. Promoter

The promoter is David Joseph. Contact: Hackneyleytonfootball@gmail.com.

2. Competition period and territory

The competition is open from 11 October 2026 until 31 May 2027 and is available to eligible residents of the United Kingdom.

3. Eligibility

Entrants must be aged 18 or over, reside in the United Kingdom and hold one valid Hackney Super 6 account. Organisers may refuse or disqualify entries involving fraud, multiple accounts, abusive conduct, manipulation or a breach of these rules.

4. Free entry

Entry is free. No purchase, stake, entry fee or in-app payment is required. Internet access and a compatible device may be needed.

5. How to enter a round

Each published round contains six fixtures. Enter a whole-number home and away score from 0 to 99 for every fixture and save all six predictions before the published deadline. Predictions saved before the deadline may be changed and saved again before that deadline. The last valid saved prediction is used. Entries received after the deadline do not count.

6. Scoring

  • 6 points for an exact score;
  • 3 points for the correct match result (home win, draw or away win) without the exact score;
  • 0 points otherwise.

The maximum ordinary round score is 36 points.

7. Results source

The “authoritative results source” means the official match result accepted and recorded by the Hackney & Leyton Football League or, where necessary, the official competition record confirmed by the organiser from reliable league information. If that record is formally corrected, the organiser may correct the app’s result and recalculate affected scores.

8. Postponed fixtures and replacements

Before the deadline, an organiser may replace a postponed fixture and users may need to review their predictions. A fixture postponed after the deadline is not counted. Because the round then has fewer than six countable fixtures, six exact scores cannot be achieved; five exact scores do not create a competition winner for that round.

9. Cancelled rounds

A cancelled round is removed from the competition. Its results and points do not contribute to weekly or season standings.

10. Standings and ties

The app ranks players by points, then exact scores, then correct results; players who remain level share the displayed place. If a separate season reward must be allocated between level players, the order is determined first by the greatest total number of exact scores predicted during the season, and then by the greatest total number of correct predictions achieved in the shortest elapsed period. If the tie still cannot be resolved objectively from the records, the promoter will publish a fair, independently reviewable resolution before awarding the reward.

11. Reward

The selected eligible user receives an opportunity for their chosen team in the Hackney & Leyton Football League to receive free use of pitches for 28 days during the current season, beginning on a date chosen by the recipient, subject to venue availability and reasonable booking conditions. The reward is not cash, is not transferable for value and has no cash alternative. Its approximate value depends on the applicable pitch hire charges for the selected 28-day period.

12. Claiming

The promoter will contact the selected recipient using the registered contact route. The recipient must respond within seven calendar days. If the recipient does not respond within that period, the opportunity is lost and cannot be reinstated.

13. Technical failures and fair administration

The promoter is not responsible for device, network or service failures outside reasonable control. An entry exists only when it is successfully saved. The promoter may pause, correct or cancel a round when necessary to protect fairness, security or the integrity of the competition, and will retain an audit record of organiser actions.

14. Privacy

Personal information is handled under the Privacy Policy. Information necessary to administer eligibility, scoring, moderation and the reward may be used for those purposes.

15. Apple and Google

Apple Inc. and Google LLC do not sponsor, endorse, administer or have any involvement with Hackney Super 6 or its competition. Entrants release Apple and Google from claims arising solely from administration of the competition to the extent permitted by law.

16. Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Nothing in these rules removes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Important: These published rules describe the free competition. They do not create any paid entry or gambling facility.
