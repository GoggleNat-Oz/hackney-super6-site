Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 5 October 2026

David Joseph operates Hackney Super 6 and is the data controller for personal information handled through the app. Questions and rights requests can be sent to Hackneyleytonfootball@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • Account information: email address, email-and-password sign-in record and account identifier.
  • Profile information: display name, optional team, privacy preference and notification preferences.
  • Competition information: saved predictions, prediction history, points, scoring breakdowns, eligibility and achievements.
  • Public leaderboard information: display name, a short code derived from a randomly generated player identifier, optional team, points and rank unless the player has chosen to hide from the leaderboard.
  • Administration and safety information: moderation state, audit history, organiser actions and account-deletion records.
  • Technical information: information needed by Firebase to provide authentication and database services and limited operational or security logs.

Hackney Super 6 does not use advertising, sell personal information or take payments. Push notifications and advertising tracking are not enabled.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure accounts, save predictions, calculate scores and standings, publish the leaderboard, administer rounds, prevent misuse, answer support requests, meet deletion requests and maintain an accurate sporting record.

Depending on the activity, the legal basis is performance of the service requested by the user, our legitimate interests in running a fair and secure free competition, consent where a genuine choice is offered, or compliance with legal obligations.

Service providers and international processing

The app uses Google Firebase and Google Cloud services for email-and-password authentication and Firestore data storage. Those providers process information on our behalf under their terms and safeguards. Processing may take place outside the United Kingdom; where it does, applicable contractual and legal transfer safeguards are relied upon.

Account deletion

You can request deletion in the app through More → Privacy & Data → Delete account, or follow the external instructions on the Account Deletion page.

Your email-and-password sign-in is removed when deletion is confirmed in the app. The app immediately removes your profile, public display details and any saved predictions it can safely match to protected scoring copies. Other predictions and linked records wait for a reviewed manual cleanup. We aim to complete every valid request within 30 calendar days.

Past competition records may be retained only after links to the name, email, team and sign-in account are removed. They appear as “Anonymous player”. Scores might still be recognisable to somebody who already knew them.

Your rights

Subject to UK data-protection law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email the support address to exercise a right. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

Hackney Super 6 is intended only for people aged 18 or over.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The current version and date will remain on this page.
