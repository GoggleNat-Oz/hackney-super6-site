Delete your Hackney Super 6 account

You can request deletion without reinstalling or opening the app. Email Hackneyleytonfootball@gmail.com from the email address used for the account, with the subject “Hackney Super 6 account deletion request”.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Hackney Super 6.
  2. Open More.
  3. Choose Privacy & Data.
  4. Choose Delete account.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps. The app may require your password and the word DELETE.

Delete through this website

If you cannot use the in-app route, email the support address from the account email. State that you want the Hackney Super 6 account and associated personal data deleted. We may ask for limited information needed to verify ownership, but we will never ask you to email your password.

What happens

  • Access ends when the confirmed in-app deletion removes the email-and-password authentication record. For an emailed request, access is removed after ownership is verified and the request is processed.
  • The app removes the profile, public display details and saved predictions that it can safely match to protected scoring copies.
  • A reviewed manual process removes or anonymises remaining linked records.
  • We aim to complete every valid request within 30 calendar days. If more time is legally permitted and required, we will explain why.

Information that may remain

  • Genuinely anonymised competition scores, points, ranks and results may be retained indefinitely as the historical sporting record.
  • A minimal anonymised moderation/security record may be retained for up to 12 months.
  • A pseudonymous deletion receipt may be retained for up to 12 months.
  • Backups, manual exports and operational logs may temporarily retain identifiable information but are deleted, overwritten or allowed to expire within 30 days.
  • Information may be retained where the law specifically requires it.

See the Privacy Policy for further details or contact Hackneyleytonfootball@gmail.com for the status of a request.
