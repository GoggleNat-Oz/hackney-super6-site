Support

Hackney Super 6 is a free football prediction app for followers of the Hackney & Leyton Football League. There are no entry fees, stakes or in-app payments.

Contact us

For help with signing in, predictions, account access, privacy, deletion requests or competition questions, email Hackneyleytonfootball@gmail.com.

Please describe the problem and, where useful, include your device type and the app screen involved. Do not send your password.

Reporting a predictor name

To report an offensive or inappropriate predictor name, email the support address and include the name exactly as it appears in the app. Organisers can review reports and apply moderation controls.

Account and privacy

You can manage your profile and deletion request in the app through More → Privacy & Data. A separate web deletion route is also available.
